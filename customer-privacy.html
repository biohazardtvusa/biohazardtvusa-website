Customer portal privacy notice

This notice describes the information used for customer sign-in and the referral program.

Information used

The portal stores your name and email address, customer-access status, referral code, referral activity, contest participation and standings, and any free-month referral credit earned. It uses short-lived email links to verify email ownership and sign you in. The browser receives a seven-day, secure, HTTP-only session cookie.

How it is used

We use this information to review customer access, provide a personal referral link, record confirmed referrals, show earned referral months, and calculate referral contest standings for dates selected by BIOHAZARDTVUSA. Referral access is approved by BIOHAZARDTVUSA after customer status is checked. A referral reward or contest count is recorded only after the business confirms the referred person is a new client.

Sign-in and verification emails are sent through Resend. Website account and referral records are stored by Netlify. The portal does not collect or store payment-card details, and online payment processing is not currently available through the portal.

Marketing is separate

Customer portal access does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Marketing signup remains a separate, optional flow that requires email confirmation and can be unsubscribed from at any time.

Questions or removal requests

To request access, correction, or removal of customer portal information, email Biohazardtvusa@gmail.com. Removing portal access does not cancel TV service or change any referral credit already applied.

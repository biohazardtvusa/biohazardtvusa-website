Privacy notice

This notice covers messages sent through the BIOHAZARDTVUSA website contact form and the optional BIOHAZARDTVUSA / BIOHAZARD MEDIA email update list. Neither action purchases or activates live TV service.

Contact form messages

If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, message, and optional phone number so we can respond to your inquiry. The message is sent to our business inbox through Resend and is not stored in a separate website database. Submitting a contact request does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Email providers may process and retain messages under their own privacy practices.

What the form collects

  • Your email address and optional first name.
  • Your optional state or location choice. You may select “prefer not to say.” Signup is not limited to Texas.
  • The marketing consent wording, consent date and signup source.

No credit card information or payment details are requested. Choosing “prefer not to say” does not prevent signup.

How signup information is used

The list is intended for BIOHAZARDTVUSA live TV updates, offers and referral news. Marketing consent is required and is not preselected. The form does not send a confirmation email or verify ownership of the address submitted.

Storage and access

Signup records are stored in private Netlify storage and are not displayed publicly. Authorized business administrators can access the list through a time-limited secure link sent to the configured business admin email. Netlify processes the website and signup storage; Resend processes administrator access and contact emails.

For abuse prevention, the website keeps hourly counters keyed to a hash derived from the connection address. The contact form and signup application do not save your raw connection address in their records. Hosting and email providers may retain their own operational logs.

Unsubscribe or request removal

You can ask to stop receiving marketing, request correction, or request removal of your signup information by emailing Biohazardtvusa@gmail.com. Future marketing emails should also include unsubscribe instructions. Unsubscribing from marketing does not cancel TV service.

BIOHAZARDTVUSA is independently operated and is not affiliated with Biohazard TV Canada.

Still have questions?

Your live TV questions, answered.

Quick answers about compatible devices, watching on multiple screens, payments and referral rewards.

What devices can the live TV service be installed on?

Our live TV service can be installed on Android-based and Apple devices. Message us on WhatsApp Business with your device model and we'll help you get started.

Do I need multiple devices to watch on more than one TV?

Yes, if you want to watch on those TVs at the same time. Each TV needs the service installed on its own compatible device. Your live TV plan also needs enough simultaneous connections for the screens you want to watch at once.

Can I watch the live TV service on my phone?

Yes. You can watch on Android phones and iPhones.

Do you offer referral bonuses?

Yes. For each new client who signs up through your referral, you earn one free month of service after we verify the signup, set up their Xstream account, and extend your subscription.

What payment options do you accept?

Venmo and Zelle are our preferred payment options. PayPal is also available if you need it. Message us on WhatsApp Business to arrange payment.

Can I cancel my plan?

Plans are month-to-month. If you cancel, cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. For help with your account, contact us through WhatsApp or the Contact Us form.

Does BIOHAZARDTVUSA store my credit card information?

No. BIOHAZARDTVUSA does not store your credit card information, and we will never ask you to share it with us. Please do not send credit card details through our website, WhatsApp or email.

Is BIOHAZARDTVUSA affiliated with Biohazard TV Canada?

No. BIOHAZARDTVUSA operates independently and is not affiliated with Biohazard TV Canada. BIOHAZARD MEDIA is its secondary brand. The live TV plans on this website are offered by BIOHAZARDTVUSA. For our plan details or support, use our WhatsApp Business contact.
